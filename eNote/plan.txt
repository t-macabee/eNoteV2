## Qodana Remediation Plan

### Preliminary findings from code inspection

**`AccessToDisposedClosure` (DiResolutionTests.cs:34)** — Qodana false positive. `Record.Exception` runs the lambda synchronously; `scope` is alive throughout. No runtime risk. Fixable by restructuring to remove the warning without changing semantics.

**`UnusedVariable employee` (InstrumentService.cs:75/100/116)** — `EnsureStoreAccessAsync()` is called for its authorization side-effect only; the returned `MusicStoreEmployee` is discarded. Not a bug, but poorly expressed. Fix: drop the assignment, just `await EnsureStoreAccessAsync();`.

**`ParameterHidesPrimaryConstructorParameter` (RentalCommandService.cs:88)** — method param `actor` shadows the primary ctor's `actor`. Rename method param to `rentalActor`.

**`SmtpEmailService` using-statement warnings** — `using var` form already guarantees disposal regardless of when `client.Credentials` is set. Qodana is applying a rule that applies to the older `using (var x = ...)` statement form. Suppression or minor restructuring is the fix.
